About the journal

A global forum for medical discovery

The Elicit Journal of Medical Science and Discovery (EJMSD) is a peer-reviewed, open-access scholarly journal published by Elicit Publishing Limited. It publishes original research, critical reviews, clinical studies, case reports and innovative methodologies that advance the understanding, prevention, diagnosis and treatment of human disease.

EJMSD is committed to high-quality, evidence-based research that bridges laboratory discovery and clinical application, promoting health innovation, global healthcare solutions and sustainable medical practice.
